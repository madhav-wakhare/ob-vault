
A **Virtual Private Network (VPN)** is a secure communication method that creates an **encrypted tunnel** over an untrusted network (such as the Internet). It allows data to travel safely between two endpoints such as users, servers, or entire networks, while maintaining **confidentiality, integrity, and authentication**.
