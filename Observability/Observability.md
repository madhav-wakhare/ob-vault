Observability is the ability to understand a system's internal state by examining its external outputs or telemetry data. Unlike simple monitoring—which tells you _when_ something breaks—observability helps you debug _why_ it broke, making it essential for complex, distributed cloud and microservices architectures


The Three Pillars (MELT)
- **Metrics:** Numeric time-series values measuring health, CPU usage, or request rates.
- **Events:** Distinct, timestamped occurrences tied to system changes.
- **Logs:** Discrete textual or structured records of specific software events.
- **Traces:** End-to-end paths of single requests as they flow across multiple microservices.
