## Key Takeaways

- [**Prioritize Customer Outcomes:** Customers care about business value (e.g., uptime, revenue), not your tech stack. Align your work with their goals to avoid building features that lead to churn.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1140.0 "PLAY @19:00")
- [**Master Communication:** Quality work is insufficient. You must provide quality service by proactively communicating value, managing expectations, and enabling customers to champion your work.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=2541.0 "PLAY @42:21")
- [**Act as a Bridge:** Represent both your company and the customer. This dual ownership empowers you to find workarounds and escalate issues to ensure customer success, even when product delivery is delayed.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1587.0 "PLAY @26:27")
- [**Know Your Counterpart:** Understand your customer contact's KPIs and promotion criteria. Frame your work to help them succeed, making your product or service indispensable to their career.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1710.0 "PLAY @28:30")

## Topics

### The Engineering-to-CS Mindset Shift

- [Saurabh's transition from SRE to CS at Last9 was a reality check:](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=840.0 "PLAY @14:00")
    - [**Initial Focus:** Deep technical work (e.g., Kubernetes networking, building a dynamic service map).](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=926.0 "PLAY @15:26")
    - [**Customer Reality:** Customers cared only about business outcomes (e.g., uptime for a major sale), not the underlying tech.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1075.0 "PLAY @17:55")
- [**Core Realization:** A customer's business reality is constant, while tech stacks change.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1219.0 "PLAY @20:19")
- [**Key Question:** Do you care what tech stack Uber runs if you don't get your cab? No. Customers only care if the product solves their problem.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1140.0 "PLAY @19:00")

    - [**Lesson:** No one cares about your "cool" tech unless it solves a specific problem.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1402.0 "PLAY @23:22")
- [**Customer 2 (CEO Deemed Failure):** Talked 90% of the time, overwhelming the customer.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1445.0 "PLAY @24:05")

- [**Customer 4 (Churned):** Pushed priorities on a customer contact whose KPIs had changed.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=1710.0 "PLAY @28:30")

### Practical Frameworks & Tactics

- [**Quality Work vs. Quality Service (Chinmay's Mechanic Analogy):**](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=2541.0 "PLAY @42:21")
    - [**Quality Work:** Surprise bill for necessary repairs.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=2555.0 "PLAY @42:35")
    - [**Quality Service:** Proactive inspection, clear explanation, and a choice for the customer.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=2640.0 "PLAY @44:00")
    - [**Outcome:** The latter builds trust, even if it costs more.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=2763.0 "PLAY @46:03")
- [**N-1 Prep:** Complete all action items the day _before_ a customer meeting to ensure readiness.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=3000.0 "PLAY @50:00")
- [**Silent QBRs:** Internally evaluate each customer: Will they stay or churn? If they stay, do they know the value you provide?](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=3079.0 "PLAY @51:19")
- [**The "Tali, Gali, or Standup" Demo Test:**](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=3180.0 "PLAY @53:00")
    - [**Tali (Clap):** Success. The customer sees value.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=3180.0 "PLAY @53:00")
    - [**Gali (Scold):** Failure. The product or demo missed the mark.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=3180.0 "PLAY @53:00")
    - [**Standup (Silence):** Most dangerous. The customer has given up.](https://fathom.video/share/sYWKNLYj5zmT33kH_Fi6ZSaQLbkqcadv?tab=summary&timestamp=3180.0 "PLAY @53:00")
